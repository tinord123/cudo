# University of Toronto Academic-Unit Political Statements

Statements **attributed to an academic unit itself** (department / institute / centre / school / college), on contested political or social issues. Excludes: individual faculty signatures, student unions/councils, faculty advocacy chapters (Faculty for Palestine, Jewish Faculty Network), and central administration (President, Provost, VP offices).

**Already known to the requester (not re-catalogued in full below):** Women & Gender Studies Institute (WGSI) Palestine statement; Centre for Criminology & Sociolegal Studies; Health Studies (UTSC). *Note: an independent search did not surface a unit-attributed solidarity statement on the Centre for Criminology's own site — only co-sponsored events (e.g. "Hearing Palestine" seminars). Worth confirming which Criminology item you saw.*

**Liveness method:** Direct HTTP check where possible. Six `*.utoronto.ca` department subdomains (anthropology, geography, eas, history, sociology, religion, cdtps) sit behind a Web Application Firewall that returns **HTTP 403 to all automated tools** (and to the homepage and to nonsense URLs alike) — so 403 ≠ removed. For those, liveness was confirmed via the Internet Archive's most-recent capture and its HTTP status (the Archive's crawler is allowlisted by UofT). "Live (WAF)" = open in a normal browser to view. Verified as of **2026-06-25**.
